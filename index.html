CS 460/560 · AUTONOMOUS ROBOTICS · LECTURE 07

A robot never perceives the world. It reconciles competing estimates.

Every number a robot acts on is a belief: a prediction it already held, nudged by a measurement it does not fully trust.

SENSOR FUSION · PREDICT · CORRECT · THE GAIN K
02 · THE PROBLEM

Two estimates. Neither one is the truth.

One sensor answers instantly and jitters. The other reports the shape of the motion faithfully but arrives late.

Averaging them does not give you the truth. It gives you a third wrong answer, with no record of how wrong.

— TRUTH (NEVER OBSERVED)  ·  ● NOISY SENSOR  ·  – – DELAYED SENSOR
03 · THE WHOLE IDEA, ONE LINE

Keep a belief. Predict it forward. Correct it with evidence.

Two steps, repeated at every tick of the clock. Nothing else in the filter is new.

04 · THE GAIN IS THE WHOLE CONTROLLER

One number decides how much of the sensor you swallow.

x̂ = x̄ + K( z − x̄ )
RESIDUAL ( z − x̄ ) — HOW WRONG THE PREDICTION TURNED OUT TO BE
x̄predicted state, before the sensor speaks zwhat the sensor actually reported Kgain, 0 → 1 : the fraction of the gap you accept

The residual is the only new information in the update. Everything the filter does is decide what fraction of it to believe.

Set K = 0 and the sensor is ignored. Set K = 1 and the prediction is thrown away. Every useful robot lives strictly in between.

05 · WHAT K BUYS, WHAT K COSTS

K buys responsiveness and pays for it in noise.

K ≈ 1

Trust the sensor

Follows every real change the instant it happens — and inherits every bit of the sensor's jitter. Control built on it chatters.

K ≈ 0

Trust the model

Beautifully smooth — and blind to anything the model did not predict. A disturbance is noticed only after the damage.

06 · WHERE K COMES FROM

K is not a taste setting. It falls out of the two variances.

K = σ²pred ⁄ ( σ²pred + σ²meas )
Sensor gets noisier
σ²meas ↑  ⟹  K → 0

The reading is barely evidence, so keep the belief you already had.

Sensor gets cleaner
σ²meas ↓  ⟹  K → 1

Snap to the measurement; your model has nothing better to offer.

Model gets shakier
σ²pred ↑  ⟹  K → 1

You have lost the state, so let the sensor pull you back onto it.

Model gets solider
σ²pred ↓  ⟹  K → 0

Hold the belief; one surprising reading should not move you far.

07 · THE TWO STEPS, HONESTLY ACCOUNTED

Confidence decays while you predict and recovers when you measure.

Prediction is where the physics lives: the motion model carries the state forward, and the model's own error inflates the variance.

Each measurement pulls that variance back down. The filter is therefore not just an estimate — it is an estimate plus a running account of how much to trust it.

08 · WHERE YOU WILL MEET IT

Same two steps. Four different pairs of sensors.

Failure modes chosen not to overlap
01Mobile robot
IMU + WHEEL ODOMETRY

Odometry is smooth but drifts with every slip; the IMU is fast but noisy. Fused, heading stays honest between landmarks.

02Drone
GPS + INERTIAL

Inertial integration is smooth and drifts without bound; GPS is absolute and jumps. One corrects exactly what the other gets wrong.

03Wearable
9-AXIS IMU + EMG RIG

Gyroscope, accelerometer and magnetometer each fail differently, so joint angle comes from the fused estimate — never from one axis.

04Tracking
CAMERA DETECTION + MOTION MODEL

The detector drops frames and occasionally hallucinates. The filter fills the gaps and refuses the physically implausible jump.

A filter is not a trick for merging sensors. It is a rule for how much evidence it takes to change your mind.
